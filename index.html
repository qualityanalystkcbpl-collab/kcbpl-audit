📄 Privacy Policy

Last Updated: August 2026

Welcome to the QA Control Room & Analytics Portal. Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, and protected while using this portal.

1. Purpose

This portal is designed exclusively for GTPL KCBPL employees to monitor and manage Call Quality Performance, audit reports, analytics, and quality-related activities.

2. Information We Collect

The portal may collect and display the following information:

  • Employee Name
  • Employee ID
  • Department / Process
  • Team Leader Information
  • Call Quality Scores
  • Audit Reports
  • Performance Metrics
  • Login Activity
  • Portal Usage Duration
  • AI-generated Feedback
  • Other quality-related information

3. How We Use Your Information

The collected information is used only for:

  • Quality monitoring
  • Performance evaluation
  • Coaching and feedback
  • Reporting and analytics
  • Management review
  • Process improvement
  • Internal operational purposes

4. Data Security

Reasonable security measures are implemented to protect information stored within the portal. Access is restricted based on user roles and permissions.

5. Confidentiality

All information available in this portal is strictly confidential. Users are prohibited from:

  • Sharing portal data with unauthorized persons.
  • Downloading or distributing confidential information without approval.
  • Using portal information for personal or non-business purposes.

6. User Responsibilities

Every user is responsible for:

  • Keeping login credentials confidential.
  • Logging out after completing work.
  • Reporting any unauthorized access immediately.
  • Using the portal only for official business purposes.

7. AI-Generated Information

Some sections of this portal use Artificial Intelligence (AI) to generate insights, recommendations, and performance summaries. AI-generated content is provided for informational purposes only and should not be considered the sole basis for performance evaluation. Final decisions remain with the Quality Team and Management.

8. Third-Party Services

This portal may utilize trusted third-party services such as:

  • Google Sheets
  • Google API
  • GitHub Pages

These services process data only to support the functionality of the portal.

9. Data Accuracy

Users are encouraged to report any incorrect information or discrepancies to the Quality Team for verification and correction.

10. Policy Updates

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically without prior notice. Continued use of the portal constitutes acceptance of the latest version.

11. Contact

For questions, concerns, or privacy-related issues, please contact:
Quality Team
