  • Hold, Transfer ও Dead Air Guidelines কঠোরভাবে অনুসরণ করতে হবে।

QA Target KPIs

  • Conduct daily/weekly/monthly call audits of randomly selected agent calls. Conduct feedback sessions 4 calls/week/agent.
  • Planning, organising and time management skills - Has the ability to priortize tasks.
  • Monitoring and Maintaining the login hours/break timings/and extra pause timings for the team.
  • Effective communication skills - Listens and provides regular updates proactively.
  • Team work - Coordinates, co-operates and adapts with team members effectively.
  • Identify the need of training (TNI) for agents and conduct sessions in coordination with HR.
