QA Target KPIs

  • Conduct daily/weekly/monthly call audits of randomly selected agent calls.Conduct feedback call sessions with call centre teams 4 calls /week / agent
  • Planning,organising and time management skills- Has the ability to plan and priortize tasks to perform within timelines
  • Monitoring and Maintaining the login hours/break timings/and extra pause timings for the team as per the specified target
  • Effective communciation skills-Listens and understand the work delegated by supervisor and provides regular update pro actively
  • Team work- Co-ordinates, co-operates, accomodates and adapts with team members effectively.
  • Identify the need of training (TNI) for the different agents from all teams and conduct the same in coordination with HR for improvement in call quality
